A11y Intuition Lab

Play. Reflect. Design for everyone.

Related projects →

A11y Intuition Lab helps students and IT professionals develop the knowledge, skills, attitudes, and habits to make digital accessibility part of everyday practice. We create open, hands-on learning tools and experiences that make human variation, context, and digital barriers tangible — helping people explore different perspectives, understand why accessibility matters, practise improving designs, and build the intuition to notice and prevent barriers before they become part of a product.

Tools

  • Gapplings

    Perspective cards for exploring how designs create barriers — Vision, Hearing, Motor, Cognitive, Memory, Attention, Language, Pace.

  • A11y Fidget Tokens

    Tactile building blocks for exploring accessibility through touch and play.

  • Gap Coin

    Memento Intervalli — a physical reminder that ability varies, and good design narrows the gap.
