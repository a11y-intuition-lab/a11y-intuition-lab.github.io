Related Projects

Digital accessibility education is a shared effort, not something any one lab or project owns. The curricula, toolkits, labs and playgrounds below have inspired our thinking or are doing related work we admire — we aren't affiliated with any of them, but we're glad they exist, and we hope you'll explore what they've built too.

Curricula & Teaching

  • W3C WAI Curricula

    Free, reusable teaching modules on digital accessibility from the Web Accessibility Initiative.

  • Teach Access

    A collaboration between industry, academia and disability advocates to build accessibility skills into higher education.

  • Teaching Accessibility (University of Southampton)

    A research project on how digital accessibility is taught and learned in academia and the workplace.

  • AccessComputing

    University of Washington initiative increasing participation of people with disabilities in computing.

  • Open curriculum for teaching digital accessibility

    Frontiers article on an open, role-based curriculum for teaching accessibility skills.

Guidelines & Toolkits

  • Microsoft Inclusive Design

    Microsoft's toolkit and principles for designing with human variation in mind from the start.

  • IBM Equal Access Toolkit

    Guidance and tools for building accessibility into every phase of product development.

  • DWP Accessibility Manual

    UK government guidance and best practice for building and testing accessible public services.

Learning Hubs & References

  • The A11Y Project

    A community-driven effort with checklists, articles and resources that make accessibility easier.

  • WebAIM

    Long-running, widely cited web accessibility research, training and evaluation resource.

  • Deque University

    Structured courses and reference material on accessible design and development.

Research Labs & Praxis

  • CanDARE — Transformative Digital Accessibility Praxis

    Research and resources reframing digital accessibility as ongoing praxis rather than pass/fail compliance.

  • Penn State A11y Lab

    HCI research group building assistive technology and accessible computing systems.

  • Accessibility Lab (a11ylab.com)

    Web accessibility audits, training and consulting focused on WCAG conformance.

  • Access Learning Lab — Accessifiers

    Tech skills and learning programs built for Deaf students and communities.

  • Inclusive Interaction Lab — UC Merced

    Research into natural, intuitive interaction techniques that make computing accessible to everyone.

Playgrounds & Interactive Tools

  • A11y Playground — GovTech Singapore

    Checklists, stories and interactive games that turn accessibility knowledge into action.

  • Meeple Like Us

    Research annotations and accessibility reviews of tabletop games, plus the Tabletop Accessibility Guidelines.
